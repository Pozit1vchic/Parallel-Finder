# Correction: Butcher identity review

Earlier versions of this document and the Butcher identity controls mistakenly labelled the clean-shaven Mother's Milk appearance (track 24, 15.89 s, and track 1029, 642.20 s) as A-Train, and called its grouping with bearded Mother's Milk a false merge. We withdraw that finding. A broader contact sheet and closer review show the same later-season face and costume across tracks 24, 114, 416, 797 and 1029. The 114/416/797 views show the Fat Joe shirt associated with Mother's Milk ([reference](https://creativetdesign.com/product/the-boys-mothers-milk-fat-joe-shirt/)); the actor lost weight and shaved between seasons ([background](https://thedirect.com/article/mothers-milk-recast-actors-the-boys-season-4-change)). This is a correction to our labels, not a claim that all tracks in the group are verified.

The four erroneous negative pairs in `butcher-bridge-controls.json` are now labelled positive. With v16 frozen tracks the corrected nine pairs are all joined; the rejected split experiment joins five and splits four. The corrected 15-case Butcher fixture joins 12 positives, splits one, and separates both negatives. The unrelated Kyle controls and the user's statement that the two women are different remain unchanged. These few, overlapping, hand-selected controls do not establish whole-video accuracy, and extensive unresolved coverage remains.

Track identifiers apply only to the saved local run; no footage or embeddings are included. Use `pf_character_probe --frozen-tracks` and `tools/benchmark/evaluate_identity_controls.py` to reproduce. `butcher-milk-appearance-controls.json` adds 60 correlated same-person pairs from the broader appearance sheet; v16 joins all of them. They add coverage of that character but no new negative controls and cannot estimate overall precision. Further independent negatives and whole-group review are needed before claiming the grouping is reliable.

The prior face-model comparison used the same incorrect label: SFace cosines 0.578 for tracks 24/1029 and 0.642 for 60/1029 are both same-person examples, not overlapping positive/negative evidence. AuraFace on 24/1029 was 0.213. No model was changed based on that experiment.
