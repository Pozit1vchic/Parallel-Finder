# Investigation of an identity bridge in the Butcher montage

The user's supplied montage exposes a serious transitive grouping failure: A-Train and Mother's Milk are grouped together. The development fixture `tests/fixtures/butcher-bridge-controls.json` records nine visually reviewed pairs from six face observations. It supplements, and partly overlaps, the main Butcher controls; the pairs are correlated and cannot be treated as independent population accuracy measurements.

For frozen v16 observations, all four different-person pairs are incorrectly joined, while all five same-person pairs are joined. An experimental stricter conflict rule for larger faces separated some displayed appearances but did not separate the 60/182-second bridge from A-Train: the four different-person pairs remained joined and four of the five same-person pairs became split. The experiment was not adopted. In particular, improving the original small set of reported negative pairs did not establish that the mixed candidate was repaired.

We also tested one face crop per track with YuNet alignment and SFace and AuraFace locally. On the original 4K crops, SFace cosine was 0.578 for same-person tracks 24 and 1029, compared with 0.642 for different-person tracks 60 and 1029; AuraFace cosine for 24 and 1029 was 0.213. Crops were independently detected within the recorded person boxes. One image per view is insufficient to rank models or prove recognition accuracy, but these examples do not support a simple threshold or a claim that higher-resolution face crops alone solve the problem. See `identity-model-comparison.md` for model provenance and licensing limitations.

Track identifiers are local to the saved run, not stable person identifiers. Reproduce clustering checks with `pf_character_probe --frozen-tracks` on the saved Butcher observations and scene log, followed by `tools/benchmark/evaluate_identity_controls.py` on this and the other fixtures. The source video, models and saved observations are private local artifacts, not included in this repository. A successful fix must also demonstrate that all constituent tracks of each confirmed group belong together, using broader independent labels and real application runs; these handpicked controls alone cannot establish that.
